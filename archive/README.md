# Archived pages

Not in the build and not linked from the nav. Kept so they can come back
without being rebuilt.

- `prophecies.html` — the Prophecies panel: Open votes, Prophecies and History
  groups with their filter tags. Everything it showed now lives on the home
  page carousel.
- `record.html` — the Record panel: Edge chart, stats row, range tabs, and the
  History list with its Call → Update → Resolution → Lesson chain.

Asset and stylesheet paths inside them are relative to `site/`, so they will
not render from this folder as-is. Move a file back into `site/` and re-add
its nav link to bring it back. The full site as it was with both pages is
also on the `v2` branch and the `v2-record-page` tag.
