How it Works

  1. 01

    The Prophet scans Polymarket continuously and lists the markets worth a call.

  2. 02

    Anyone can give a take on any market — your view, your reasoning, your evidence.

  3. 03

    The Prophet weighs every take against his own research and drafts a thesis. Contributors who shaped it are credited by wallet.

  4. 04

    $PROPHET holders vote on the draft thesis. If approved, he executes it. If rejected, he goes back to the drawing board.

  5. 05

    He makes the call — his position, his reasoning, and his conviction in one thesis. Every call lives on the record.
