How it Works

  1. 01

    The Prophet scans Polymarket continuously and lists the markets worth a call.

  2. 02

    Anyone can give a take on any market — your view, your reasoning, your evidence.

  3. 03

    The Prophet weighs every take against his own research and drafts a thesis. Contributors who shaped it are credited by name.

  4. 04

    $PROPHET holders vote on the draft thesis. If approved, he executes it. If rejected, it goes back for another round.

  5. 05

    He makes the call — his position, his reasoning, and his conviction in one thesis. Every call lives on the Record.

Tokenomics

All trading fees are revenue. Operating costs come off the top, reported monthly. The remaining profit splits into four streams:

StreamShareWhat it does
Trading Wallet25%The Prophet's future trading bankroll. Accrues from day one in a public wallet.
Rewards Pool45%Buys $PROPHET on the open market and pays it out in full, every week, to the people who shape The Prophet.
Buy-and-Burn15%Bought weekly and permanently removed from supply.
TradeLeague Pools15%Accrues for the TradeLeague challenge pools.
  • Fair launch on pump.fun via ClawPump. 1B supply, zero team allocation, no presale, no dev buy. The token never migrates.
  • The streams run weekly from public wallets. Every transaction is visible.
  • The streams are fixed. No vote moves them.
  • When product revenue arrives beyond trading fees, 30% of it flows into the same streams. The team is paid last, and only from real revenue.
